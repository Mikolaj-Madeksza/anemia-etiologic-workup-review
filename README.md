# Etiologic investigation of anemia in medical inpatients

Materials for a scoping review of 114 reports: 26 primary and 88 contextual.

- [protocol.md](protocol.md): review protocol.
- [search/](search/): database search strategies and PubMed, Scopus and Web of Science exports from 7 November 2026.
- [full-text-screening-decisions.csv](full-text-screening-decisions.csv): inclusion, exclusion and unavailable-report decisions.
- [data-extraction.xlsx](data-extraction.xlsx): study characteristics and findings, with original-report page, table, figure or section locations and source links for the additions.

NR means not reported; NA means not applicable. Counts refer to reports, not necessarily independent cohorts.

Screening codes: P = primary; S = contextual; E1 = excluded for report type; E2 = excluded for lack of relevant investigation-practice data; N = not retrieved. There are 11 full-text exclusions, 5 reports not retrieved and no pending eligibility decisions.
